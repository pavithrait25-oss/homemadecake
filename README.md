## 🎂 3D Chocolate Birthday Cake Animation Features

* **Gourmet Chocolate Styling:**
  * Double-tiered chocolate sponge with rich Belgian dark chocolate glaze.
  * Mirror ganache glaze with organic dripping ganache drips cascading down both tiers.
  * Buttercream rosettes with plump candied ruby cherries and stems.
  * Birthday candles with 3D wicks and glowing animated flickering flames.
  * Point light casting warm candlelight reflections across the chocolate glaze.
  * Golden celebration topper pick ("Sri Cakes" star medallion).
  * Porcelain pedestal stand with gold accent rims and realistic ambient contact shadows.
* **Floating Chocolate & Confectionery Elements:**
  * 8 multi-faceted chocolate shards / chunks rotating on multiple axes and floating with sinusoidal bobbing.
  * 6 glossy chocolate ribbon shavings / curls in gentle orbit.
  * 12 high-shine metallic golden confectionery pearls floating at staggered depths.
  * 24 subtle golden confectionery sparkle particles drifting with upward bakery aura.
* **Smooth Controls & Interaction:**
  * Continuous gentle auto-rotation like a luxury pastry shop display turntable.
  * Interactive 360° mouse drag (desktop) and touch drag (mobile) with smooth inertia.
  * Subtle cursor hover parallax camera motion.
* **Performance & Accessibility:**
  * **Zero Lag:** 100% procedural 3D math geometry (loads in milliseconds, 0MB model files).
  * **Intersection Observer:** Automatically pauses render loop when user scrolls away from the hero section, saving 100% GPU/CPU on battery.
  * **Reduced Motion:** Fully respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` settings.
  * **Offline Support:** Includes local `three.min.js` so it works offline with zero external network dependencies.
  * **Graceful Fallback:** If WebGL is disabled or unsupported, falls back smoothly to the high-resolution chocolate cake image.

## ⚙️ Quick Baker Customization Feature

To make it effortless for the business owner to update details:
1. Scroll to the footer and click the **“⚙️ Customize Baker Details”** button.
2. Enter your real WhatsApp phone number and bakery address.
3. Click **“Save Details”**.
4. The entire website (all WhatsApp links, phone calling links, and address text) will update immediately and persist in your browser!

## 🚀 How to Open the Website

1. Simply double-click **`index.html`** or right-click and choose **Open with > Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Safari**.
2. No installation, server, or database is required—it runs instantly in any modern web browser!
