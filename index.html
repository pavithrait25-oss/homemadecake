Our Story & Passion

Freshly Baked with Love at Sri Homemade Cakes

Welcome to Sri Homemade Cakes! What began as a heartfelt passion for baking joyous treats for family and friends has blossomed into a cherished home bakery serving our local community.

Unlike commercial bakeries that mass-produce cakes with chemical emulsifiers, artificial essences, and refrigerated days-old sponges, Sri Homemade Cakes is built on one simple belief: a cake should taste just as fresh, comforting, and wholesome as if made in your mother’s kitchen.

🍰

100% Baked on Confirmed Order

Your oven never heats up until your order is confirmed. You receive an ultra-soft, freshly baked crumb every single time.

🧈

Honest, High-Quality Ingredients

We use genuine dairy butter, rich Dutch cocoa, farm-fresh fruit compotes, and premium cream cheese. No shortcuts, no compromise.

🌱

Specialists in Vegetarian & Eggless Cakes

Looking for a pure vegetarian celebration cake? Our eggless sponges are naturally moist, light, and adored by kids and elders alike.
